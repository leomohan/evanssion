Overview

Why it matters in MEA

AI adoption in MEA is accelerating faster than security teams can keep pace. LLM prompt injection, AI agent compromise, shadow AI, and data leakage through AI tools represent an entirely new attack surface. Evanssion is the region's first VAD with a dedicated AI Security practice.

Request a briefing →

Prompt injection

Adversaries manipulate AI models through crafted inputs to bypass controls or exfiltrate data.

Shadow AI

Employees use unauthorised AI tools, exposing sensitive corporate data to third-party models.

Agent-to-agent attacks

Multi-agent systems create new attack paths as malicious instructions propagate between agents.

AI governance gaps

No visibility into what AI tools are used, by whom, and what data they process.
