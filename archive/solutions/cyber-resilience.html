Overview

Why it matters in MEA

Building defenses is only half the equation. Knowing they work — under real attack conditions — is what separates resilient organisations from victims. Evanssion's cyber resilience practice helps MEA enterprises continuously validate their controls, train their teams, and measure their true security posture.
