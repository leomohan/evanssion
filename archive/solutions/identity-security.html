Overview

Why it matters in MEA

Identity is the new perimeter. Over 80% of breaches in MEA involve compromised credentials or privilege abuse. Evanssion delivers identity security solutions that protect every user, service account, and machine identity — from privileged access management to zero trust network access.

Request a briefing →

Credential theft

Phishing, credential stuffing, and social engineering remain the top attack vectors in the region.

Privilege abuse

Overprivileged accounts and lateral movement allow attackers to escalate from low-level access to domain admin.

Machine identities

Proliferating service accounts, API keys, and certificates create unmanaged identity sprawl.

Remote workforce

Hybrid work increases exposure — traditional VPN provides excessive implicit trust.
