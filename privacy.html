Evanssion is a business-to-business (B2B) company. We primarily process data relating to business professionals, channel partners, and vendor contacts. We do not sell personal data to third parties and never have.

1. Who We Are

Evanssion FZCO ("Evanssion", "we", "us", or "our") is a Value-Added Distributor (VAD) specialising in cybersecurity and AI solutions across the Middle East and Africa. We are incorporated as a Free Zone Company (FZCO) in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Evanssion is the data controller for personal data collected through this website (evanssion.com) and through our business activities.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data you provide directly

  • Full name and job title
  • Business email address and telephone number
  • Company name, industry, and country
  • Messages, enquiries, and correspondence sent to us
  • Event registration details
  • CV and employment information submitted for career opportunities

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, and navigation paths on our website
  • Referring URLs and search terms
  • Cookie and tracking data (see Section 10)

2.3 Data from third parties

  • Professional profile information from LinkedIn and other public business networks
  • Business contact information from our vendor and channel partner companies
  • Event attendee information from conference and exhibition organisers

3. How We Collect Data

We collect personal data through the following channels:

  • Website forms — contact forms, enquiry forms, and newsletter sign-ups
  • Email and telephone — when you contact us directly
  • Events and exhibitions — including GITEX, GISEC, and Black Hat MEA
  • Business cards and introductions — during in-person meetings
  • LinkedIn and social media — professional outreach and engagement
  • Vendor and partner onboarding — as part of commercial agreements

4. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries and providing information about our solutions and services
  • Managing vendor partnerships and channel partner relationships
  • Sending relevant cybersecurity and AI industry updates, research, and event invitations
  • Processing job applications and managing recruitment
  • Improving our website and digital content
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Fraud prevention, security, and risk management
  • Fulfilling contractual obligations with our business partners

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, "UAE PDPL") and applicable international frameworks, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — where you have explicitly agreed to receive marketing communications or specific processing activities
  • Contractual necessity — where processing is required to fulfil a contract or pre-contractual steps at your request
  • Legitimate interests — for B2B relationship management, business development, and security activities, where these do not override your rights
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by applicable UAE or international law

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your personal data with:

  • Our vendor partners — where you have expressed interest in a specific technology solution (limited to relevant contact details)
  • Channel partners and resellers — to facilitate sales and technical engagements you have requested
  • Service providers — including cloud hosting, email marketing, analytics, and CRM platforms acting as data processors on our behalf
  • Legal and regulatory authorities — where required by UAE law, court order, or regulatory requirement
  • Group companies — including Novaala ME FZCO and associated entities, under equivalent data protection standards

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

7. International Data Transfers

Evanssion operates across the Middle East and Africa. Your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the UAE, including within our vendor and partner ecosystem across the MEA region.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including contractual data protection clauses, and that recipient countries provide an adequate level of protection consistent with UAE PDPL requirements.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law:

  • Business contacts and enquiries — up to 3 years from last interaction
  • Active partner and vendor relationships — duration of the relationship plus 3 years
  • Job applications (unsuccessful) — 12 months unless you consent to longer retention
  • Website analytics data — up to 26 months in aggregated or anonymised form
  • Legal and compliance records — as required by UAE commercial and tax law (typically 5–10 years)

9. Your Rights

Under the UAE PDPL and applicable international data protection law, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right of rectification — to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right of erasure — to request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations
  • Right to restrict processing — to request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@evanssion.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may request verification of your identity before processing your request.

10. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors use our site. We use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies — to understand traffic patterns and page performance (e.g. Google Analytics or Cloudflare Analytics)
  • Preference cookies — to remember your settings and choices

You can control cookie settings through your browser settings at any time. Disabling non-essential cookies will not affect your ability to use the website. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

11. Data Security

As a cybersecurity company, we apply rigorous security standards to protect personal data. Our measures include:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit via our website (HTTPS)
  • Access controls and authentication for all internal systems
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability reviews
  • Staff training on data protection and security awareness
  • Incident response procedures for data breaches

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify the relevant regulatory authority and affected individuals in accordance with UAE PDPL requirements.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are directed at business professionals and are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data relating to a minor, please contact us immediately at privacy@evanssion.com.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
